## Summary

The revised BRD addresses the key concerns raised in the previous review. Confirmed requirements, open business decisions, technical decisions, assumptions, dependencies and out-of-scope items are now clearly separated.

## Strengths Observed

1. Business journey and stakeholder responsibilities align with the source requirement.
2. Critical business ambiguities are identified without prematurely defining workflow behaviour.
3. Previous assumptions on manager ownership, stakeholder action cardinality and internal task-based orchestration have been removed.
4. Integration scope, candidate status values and security concerns are retained as unresolved.
5. BRD requirements are individually identifiable and mapped to the source requirement.
